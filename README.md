# Black Friday Christmas Crafts

1. I want you to act as a professional web designer and replicate exactly what is on this sales page, including every section, every question, every element, and the same mockup formats. I need a precise and faithful reproduction of the original page.

Reference page:

https://moldesdenatal.site/carinhas-natal/

2. I would like you to redesign and optimize this sales page while maintaining its core structure and purpose.

I want the entry-level plan to be priced at R$10.00. When customers click to purchase it, a pop-up should appear offering an irresistible bonus for an additional R$10.00, bringing the total to the same price as the Premium tier.

The Premium plan should be priced at R$37.90 and include additional exclusive bonuses.

Revamp the overall design with a Black Friday theme, incorporating more badges, icons, visual highlights, eye-catching typography, compelling headlines, and a more professional, high-converting layout.

Rewrite the page content to eliminate repetitive wording, improve readability, strengthen the sales arguments, and make the copy more persuasive.

Also, implement the following technical and visual improvements:

- Review and minimize unnecessary metadata and tracking information in the page's code without interfering with legitimate analytics, attribution, or advertising performance.

- Add a small footer disclaimer clarifying that the website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Meta.

- Optimize the page's technical structure, performance, and advertising compatibility to improve its suitability for ad recommendations without making unsupported guarantees about reach or delivery.

- Enhance the bonus section with additional relevant images, mockups, visual assets, and product previews to make the bonuses more attractive and tangible.

- Add a festive cursor animation featuring Christmas-themed elements such as candy canes, ornaments, and other seasonal decorations, while keeping the experience smooth and responsive on desktop and mobile.

The final result should look polished, modern, visually engaging, and conversion-focused, with a clear distinction between the entry-level offer, the additional bonus upsell, and the Premium package.
